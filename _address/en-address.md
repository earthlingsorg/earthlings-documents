# An Address to Everyone

We, the Earthlings team, are addressing those who see what is happening to the world and do not want to accept it.

In our documents we write carefully and with measured words. This address is different. In it we speak more sharply about what we see, what we think, and why we took up this work.

## What we see

While politicians spend months and years reconciling positions, thousands of people die under bombs, and millions live in want, hunger and disease. Wars that until recently seemed impossible go on for years, and no international organization can stop them. Ceasefires are signed and not observed. A conflict in one part of the world raises the price of fuel and bread on the other side of the planet. The arms race has returned, and nuclear powers are once again taking part in it. And the companies that create artificial intelligence themselves admit that they may lose control over what they are creating, and ask for rules that still do not exist.

Each of these troubles can be explained on its own. Together they add up to a picture that is hard not to see: the world is losing the ability to solve problems.

## Why this is happening

We do not think that bad people at the top are to blame for everything. The trouble runs deeper. The institutions that govern the world today are not coping with their functions.

Politics within states lives from one election to the next, while the problems demand decisions for decades ahead. Decisions depend more and more on the money of those who finance campaigns, pay lobbyists and own the mass media. The higher a person rises, the further they are from those whose lives their decisions change. We see the capacity for empathy declining among elites and the obsession with excess profit growing.

We believe that politics in its present form has exhausted its historical mission. The same is true of many international and state institutions, laws and agreements. All of this requires deep and radical rebuilding. But they cannot rebuild themselves from within: anyone who tries will run into the very rules that need to be changed. Among politicians and officials there are honest people who see this, but they have no instruments to change it.

Our civilization is heading straight for an iceberg. On the bridge they argue about who should stand at the wheel, and no one has been holding the wheel for a long time.

## What is missing

This system lacks the voice of people themselves. The UN Charter opens with the words "We the peoples of the United Nations", but at the UN only states speak. People from different countries want the same thing but cannot say it together, because they are counted separately, each in their own country. Millions of people on both sides of a border who do not want war do not add up to an opinion that anyone is obliged to take into account.

Petitions, marches and polls do not close this gap. They are easy to ignore, easy to fake and easy to brand as paid for. No one is obliged to believe that living people stand behind the figure, and no one is obliged to reckon with that figure.

## Why a people

We searched for a long time for a form of association that could create a common voice, and went through all the known ones. Parties fight for power within one country and become part of the very system they wanted to change. Social movements gather around one idea and fall apart when it drops out of the news. Foundations and non-governmental organizations do important work, but they depend on donors' money and each speaks in its own name. International civil networks remain circles of activists with no verifiable number of people behind them. Much of what we want to do, they could do as well. But none of these forms carries the weight of a people.

A people is the bearer of the right to self-determination. International law recognizes the right of peoples to decide their own fate, and the whole post-war order is built on this right. The voice of a people has to be reckoned with differently from the voice of an organization. An organization can be shut down, banned or bought, but a people exists as long as the people who make it up exist. That is why what we are founding is a people, transnational and voluntary.

The Earthlings people is not tied to a territory and lays claim to no one's land. People join it by their own choice, keeping their citizenship, their faith and their culture. Each person in it has one equal vote, which cannot be bought, accumulated or taken away. In this people there is no power of some people over others, so there is no one to seize it from within and nothing to seize it with.

## How we will exert influence

In our documents we write that Earthlings does not engage in politics, and that is true. We do not fight for power, do not nominate candidates, do not create parties, and do not claim a single seat in any government. We do not want to replace states and do not call for any coup.

We are creating a people in order to bring into the international and national agenda a legitimate and influential civic voice that is absent from it today. By founding a people, we are laying down a new institution: a direct transnational voice of people, with a verified living person behind it and a result that anyone can recount. This institution will become international when it begins to be reckoned with.

When confirmed people from many countries say one thing and governments decide another, everyone will see the discrepancy, and it will no longer be possible to write it off as the opinion of who knows whom. When such a voice backs a sensible proposal on peace, on hunger, on climate or on the control of technology, politicians who would themselves like to adopt it will gain support they do not have today. And when this voice gains enough weight, even those who would prefer not to hear it will have to reckon with it.

This institution already has a foundation. We have built a digital platform on which the people can live and act as one society. On it a person goes through identity verification and receives a non-transferable digital passport, which gives them one vote. Built into it are the people's bodies of self-governance: Cells, in which people come together for common work, run their own projects and vote, and the Assembly of all participants, which will begin its work after the Declaration is adopted. The shared economy of participation is kept on the digital platform, and the people has its own treasury, whose movements of funds are visible to everyone.

There are many reasonable people, sound ideas and good proposals in the world that get lost because they have nowhere to go. The Earthlings people can become the place where they are gathered, discussed, tested and turned into a common voice, and where what depends on us ourselves begins to be done together. We propose that people from different countries unite and gain an instrument for creating a modern, constructive society. An instrument they have not had until now.

## What we believe in

For centuries people united into peoples by blood, language and land. We propose uniting on a different basis: on shared values and on responsibility for a common future. We believe that a new transnational people is able to do what present institutions cannot, because it depends neither on elections, nor on money, nor on anyone's permission. We believe that it can, step by step, begin to set things right. We see no other force that could do this, and we can no longer wait for someone else to create one.

## Where we are now

This is only the beginning. The documents are written and the platform is built, but there are still few people. Much remains to be done. The registry key and the treasury key are still held by the founder, and a tool for secret voting is still being chosen. All of this is listed in the document "Where We Are Now", together with how and when it will change.

From 22 November 2026 any person may propose changes to any document, and on 3 April 2027 the Declaration will be adopted or rejected by people themselves, those who have confirmed their identity. It is not we, the authors of the documents, who found this people, but those who vote.

If you see what we see, read the Earthlings Declaration and the other documents and decide whether this speaks to you.

*The Earthlings team*

## Обращение

> Peoples need a voice that cannot be bought, intimidated, or switched off. We are founding it.

The UN Charter opens with the words "We the peoples". Yet it is states that decide there, and peoples are admitted as guests: they are heard, and the vote is taken without them. The interests of a state and the interests of a people diverge more often than it is customary to say out loud.

Those who decide are checked by their own appointees on their own budget. A check can be bought, a balance intimidated, an observer stripped of accreditation and expelled. A real check is possible only from a place that no budget, no appointment, and no permit can reach. No such place exists today.

We are building it: the Earthlings people. It is registered in no country, takes money from no budget, and cannot be switched off. This is not a struggle against power: an auditor does not struggle against a bank. It is an external check the world has not had before.

## Декларация

By the Declaration, people constitute themselves as a people: a community of those who have chosen to belong to one another and recognize that belonging mutually.

It says what we stand on, what we guarantee to every person, and what limits we set for ourselves. One person, one vote. No one holds power over another. Leaving is free at any moment.

Each person signs it for themselves. A people exists only as long as there are people who have freely confirmed that they belong to it.

## Учредительный период

A people that creates itself adopts its own rules itself. That is why the Declaration has not yet been adopted: it is an initial edition, open to proposals.

Anyone may propose a change - to the Declaration, the Charter, or any other document of the corpus. There is no need to join or to confirm your identity to do so. Every proposal is published together with the answer to it, whether accepted or declined.

Then the text is put to a vote of those who have confirmed their identity. Once adopted, the Declaration becomes the founding document of the people, and the principles of its unamendable core become irrevocable forever.

## Платформа

The platform is the daily life of the people: identity verification, a non-transferable passport, cells, votes, and the accounting of the internal economy. It is built and running: the tour opens right here.

The platform makes no decisions. It carries out the rules set by the Charter: it provides the interface, records the outcome, and puts it into effect. One person, one passport, one vote.

# German

The German corpus - official translation - is here: 25 masters translated from
the Russian masters in `ru/`, plus the Manifesto in `_manifest/de-manifest.md`.

Translated from Russian, not from English. Legal terms are taken from the
official German texts of the acts (UN Charter, both Covenants, the Universal
Declaration) and from German constitutional vocabulary, not translated back
from Russian.

What still needs a native German reader: the voice texts - the Manifesto, 03,
17, 23, 31. The legal documents are closed by the accuracy of citation and are
verifiable without a native speaker; intonation is not.

# Spanish

The Spanish corpus - official translation - is here: 25 masters translated from
the Russian masters in `ru/`, plus the Manifesto in `_manifest/es-manifest.md`.

Translated from Russian, not from English. Legal terms are quoted from the
authentic Spanish texts of the acts (UN Charter, both Covenants, the Universal
Declaration, General Assembly resolutions, the ICJ Statute), not translated
back from Russian. Spanish is an authentic language of those acts - the Charter
names it in Article 111 - so the term is a quotation rather than a rendering.

Every terminology decision, with its source, is recorded in
`_СЛОВАРЬ_РЕШЕНИЙ.md` in this directory. The decisions that had no canonical
source and had to be built are collected there separately and marked `[СЛАБОЕ]`:
those are the ones a native reader should look at first.

Two Spanish-specific points are set out in that file and matter more than they
look. `libre determinación` is the term of the acts and is used throughout;
`autodeterminación` is not used at all, because in Spanish it is first of all
the word of the Catalan and Basque dispute. And `derecho a decidir` appears
nowhere in any sense, for the same reason.

What still needs a native Spanish reader: the voice texts - the Manifesto, 03,
17, 23, 31. The legal documents are closed by the accuracy of citation and are
verifiable without a native speaker; intonation is not.
